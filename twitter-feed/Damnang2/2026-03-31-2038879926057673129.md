## 内容

On March 2, 2026, NVIDIA invested $2B in Coherent and another $2B in Lumentum. Jensen Huang wasn't just buying lasers. He was locking up the ability to make them.

If HBM was the bottleneck of 2023 to 2024, lasers are the bottleneck of 2025 to 2027.

**Key laser structures:**
- VCSEL: GaAs, 850nm, multimode fiber, 100-300m range, cheap
- DFB: InP, 1310/1550nm, single-mode fiber, km+ range

**Key modulation methods:**
- EML (DFB+EAM): Current 800G/1.6T workhorse and supply bottleneck
- CW laser + Silicon MZM: Main EML challenger
- MRM: NVIDIA's choice for TSMC COUPE CPO
- TFLN: Next-gen material for 3.2T era
